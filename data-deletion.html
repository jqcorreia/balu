Nano Ads

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nano Ads is an internal tool operated by Chuckle and Charge, Unipessoal to manage our own advertising through the Meta Marketing API. It does not offer Facebook Login and does not collect or store personal data about Facebook or Instagram users. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Requesting deletion

If you believe Nano Ads holds data about you and you want it deleted:

  1. Send an email to sa.is.pereira@gmail.com with the subject "Nano Ads data deletion request".
  2. Include enough information for us to find the data, such as your name and the Facebook or Instagram account involved.
  3. We will confirm we received your request, delete any data we hold about you, and confirm the deletion by email within 30 days.

Removing the App's access

You can also remove any app's access to your Facebook account at any time:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (under Your activity / Permissions).
  3. Find Nano Ads, select Remove, and confirm.
