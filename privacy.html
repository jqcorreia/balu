Nano Ads

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chuckle and Charge, Unipessoal ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Nano Ads (the "App"), an internal business tool that uses the Meta Marketing API.

1. What the App is

Nano Ads is a private, internal utility used only by Chuckle and Charge, Unipessoal to create and manage advertising campaigns on advertising accounts, Pages and other business assets that we own. It is operated through a Meta business system user. The App is not offered to the public, does not provide Facebook Login, and does not ask members of the public to sign in or grant it access to their accounts.

2. Information we process

The App only accesses data belonging to our own business assets on Meta, specifically:

  • Configuration of our own ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads and creatives;
  • Our own Facebook Page and Instagram account content used to build ads;
  • Aggregated, non-identifying performance metrics for our own ads (for example, impressions, clicks and spend).

The App does not collect, request or store personal data about Facebook or Instagram users, including names, email addresses, friend lists, messages or profile information.

3. How we use the information

We use the information only to create, update, pause and report on our own advertising. We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through the App with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates through the Meta Marketing API, as needed to run our advertising, and is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Storage and retention

The App does not keep a database of user data. Any operational data, such as logs of API requests about our own campaigns, is kept only as long as needed to operate and troubleshoot the App and is then deleted.

6. Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely, limited to authorised staff, and used only from systems under our control.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because the App does not process personal data about the public, we generally hold no such data. You can still contact us at any time (see below). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

8. Data deletion

See our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data relating to children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
